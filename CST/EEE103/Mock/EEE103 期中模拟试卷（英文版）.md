# EEE103 Midterm Mock Exam (English)

> **Scope**: Week 1 – Week 7 (Part 1 + Part 2)
> **Suggested time**: 90 minutes

## Section A · Concepts (10 questions, 3 marks each, 30 marks)

**A1.** Write the symbol and SI unit of charge, current, voltage, power and energy. Give the mathematical relations between current–charge and power–energy.

**A2.** What is the conventional current direction? How does it relate to the actual electron flow?

**A3.** State Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) and Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL).

**A4.** List the four types of dependent sources with their controlling/controlled quantities, and give the units of their scaling factors (if any).

**A5.** What is the passive sign convention? How do you tell whether an element is absorbing or supplying power from the sign of $p=vi$?

**A6.** State the superposition theorem. How do you turn off an independent voltage source and an independent current source? What about dependent sources?

**A7.** Write the two ideal op-amp rules and the ideal parameter values that justify them.

**A8.** Under DC steady state, what are a capacitor and an inductor equivalent to (open circuit or short circuit)? Why can't capacitor voltage and inductor current jump?

**A9.** Define the time constant $\tau$ of a first-order circuit (what is it for RC and for RL?). What fraction of the initial value remains after $5\tau$?

**A10.** State the maximum power transfer theorem. What value of $R_L$ gives the maximum power, and what is the expression for $p_{max}$?

## Section B · Calculation (8 questions, 70 marks)

### B1. (8 marks) Power & Energy
A $4\,\Omega$ resistor carries a current of $2\,\mathrm{A}$ (with the current direction satisfying the passive sign convention with respect to the resistor voltage).
Find: (a) the voltage across the resistor; (b) the power dissipated; (c) the energy dissipated in 10 s.

### B2. (8 marks) Series/Parallel & Division
The circuit has nodes **0 (reference), A and B**, with elements: a 24 V source from 0 to A (+ at A); $R_1=6\,\Omega$ from A to B; $R_2=3\,\Omega$ from A to B (in parallel with $R_1$); $R_3=6\,\Omega$ from B to 0.
Find: (a) the equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$ seen by the source; (b) the source current; (c) the current through $R_2$; (d) the voltage across $R_2$.

### B3. (9 marks) Nodal Analysis
The circuit has nodes **0 (reference), 1 and 2**. Elements: a $6\,\mathrm{A}$ current source into node 1 (from 0); a $2\,\mathrm{A}$ current source into node 2 (from 0); a $4\,\Omega$ resistor from node 1 to 0; a $2\,\Omega$ resistor from node 1 to 2; a $6\,\Omega$ resistor from node 2 to 0.
Use nodal analysis to find $v_1$ and $v_2$.

### B4. (9 marks) Superposition
Two branches are connected in parallel between nodes **A and B**:
- Branch 1: a 3 V source (+ at A) in series with a $6\,\Omega$ resistor;
- Branch 2: a 2 A current source (arrow from B to A, i.e. supplying current upward) in series with a $9\,\Omega$ resistor.

Let $i_x$ be the current through the $9\,\Omega$ resistor, defined from A to B. Use **superposition** to find $i_x$.

### B5. (10 marks) Thévenin & Maximum Power
Network (terminals a-b, load disconnected): a 12 V source (+ on top) in series with a $3\,\Omega$ resistor; from the far node of the $3\,\Omega$ resistor, one path goes through a $6\,\Omega$ resistor back to the source's negative terminal (reference, b), and the other path goes through a $7\,\Omega$ resistor to terminal a; terminal b is the reference.
Find: (a) the Thévenin equivalent $V_{TH}$ and $R_{TH}$ at terminals a-b; (b) the value of $R_L$ that draws maximum power, and that maximum power.

### B6. (9 marks) Operational Amplifier
A two-stage cascade: stage 1 is a summing amplifier with $R=20\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$, $R_f=40\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$, and inputs $v_1=1\,\mathrm{V}$, $v_2=0.5\,\mathrm{V}$; its output feeds a voltage follower (stage 2).
Find: (a) the output of stage 1; (b) the final output $v_{out}$.

### B7. (9 marks) Capacitors & Inductors
(a) A $2\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ capacitor has $v(t)=10e^{-1000t}\,\mathrm{V}$ for $t\ge0$. Find $i(t)$ and the energy stored at $t=0$.
(b) A $4\,\mathrm{H}$ inductor has $i(t)=3e^{-2t}\,\mathrm{A}$ for $t\ge0$. Find $v(t)$ and the energy stored at $t=0$.
(c) Find the equivalent capacitance of $3\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and $6\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ in series, and the equivalent inductance of $2\,\mathrm{H}$ and $3\,\mathrm{H}$ in parallel.

### B8. (8 marks) First-Order RC Complete Response
The switch has been at position A for a long time and moves to B at $t=0$.
- Position A ($t<0$): a 20 V source with $R_1=5\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ in series with $R_2=5\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ to ground; $C=100\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is across $R_2$.
- Position B ($t\ge0$): a 30 V source with $R_3=2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ in series with $R_4=3\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ to ground; $C$ is across $R_4$.

Find $v_C(t)$ for $t\ge0$ (state $\tau$, the initial value, the final value, and the full expression).
